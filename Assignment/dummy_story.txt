Once upon a time, in a far away kingdom there lived a rich man, who had three sons:
Simeon (a soldier), Tarras (a fat man), and Ivan (a fool).
The rich man had great expectations for Simeon and Tarras, while he expected nothing from Ivan. Simeon went off to war and died, Tarras' heart failed and died, and now the Ivan the foolish son was the only one left.
