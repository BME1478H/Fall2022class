Once upon a time, in a far away kingdom there lived a rich man, who had three sons:
Simeon (a soldier), Tarras (a fat man), and Ivan (a fool).
Simeon fell in love at 16, got married and got his wife pregnant before being drafted. Tarras opened the most successful flower shop in town. As for Ivan, well that's who this story is about, so let's not spoil the ending.
